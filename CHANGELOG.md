### Added
- **Finish a workout early** (#300): a member who had to leave early could only stop, or skip exercises one by one and lose the cool-down. Designs A + C from `finish-early/proposals.png`: tap the clock in the player's top bar, or pause → **Short on time? Finish early**. Coach Monkey offers three ready plans with their end time (**Express**: main lift + short cool-down; **Balanced**: accessories kept with fewer sets; **Wrap up now**: finish this exercise, then cool-down), or the member's own **10′ 15′ 20′ 30′ 45′** chips or **Done by** time; a list shows each exercise left as **Kept**, **Shorter**, **Less rest** or **Skip** before **Fit to 15 min · done 19:45**. After the fit the clock counts down in a pink capsule to the end time, a banner ("Done by 19:45 · 2 skipped · 3 sets less") offers **Undo** for 8 s, and the sheet has **Back to the full workout**. Engine `FinishEarly` (`WorkoutEngine/FinishEarly.swift`, unit tested): done sets and the current exercise stay, items behind the member count as done; trim order mobility → cardio (shorter, then skipped) → warm-up not started → rest between sets −25 % (min 45 s) → accessory sets to 2 → cool-down down to 3 min (flush and later stretches go; first stretches and breathing stay) → accessories skipped → accessory sets to 1 → main lift sets to 2 → 1. Supersets and circuits change together. The fit survives pause/resume (`ActiveWorkout.fitted/fitEnd`); Live Activity, Watch mirror and the saved record follow it. Demo screens `finish-early`, `player-fitted`.
- **The app learns what each member likes** (#299): swapping an exercise away, removing one before starting, or passing over it in a workout now teaches the app that the member doesn't like it; swapping one in, adding it and finishing all its sets teach the opposite. Each exercise gets an explainable score (`MemberTaste`, `Taste.swift`): swap in +1, swap out −1 (kept swaps ±1.5), added +1, removed −1, skipped −0.5 (only when a later exercise was done, so stopping early says nothing), finished +0.15 (capped); signals fade with a 60-day half-life, undoing an edit cancels it. Plans use it from the next new week (frozen per week in `SyncedProfile.weekTaste`, so the week in progress never reshuffles): liked exercises get up to +0.9 in the generator's pick score (variety still comes first), disliked ones down to −3 (two swaps away already outweigh freshness) and lose a template's favourite bonus; quick sessions and plan variations follow it too, variations leave disliked exercises out when anything else fits. Learned data syncs with the profile through the backend. Nothing learned = plans exactly as before (unit tested, `TasteTests`). Design A + C from `member-learning/proposals.png`: **What Monkey learned** (Training profile, last card) lists **You like** and **You avoid** with the reason ("Swapped out or removed 2× · skipped 1×") and a liked/avoided bar; tap one for **Forget this**, **Never suggest again** (out of every plan when anything else fits, never fades) or **Actually, I like it**, which apply to the rest of this week at once; **Learn from my choices** switch (off: nothing recorded, plans ignore it) and **Reset** with Undo. On Train, a new week made with learned tastes shows a coach card: "New week, made for you! I left out 2 moves you keep swapping and added more of what you like." with each change (**OUT** Leg press → Hack squat, **MORE** Incline DB press, 2 workouts), its reason and **Undo** (forgets that exercise and puts this week back), **Got it** and **What Monkey learned ›** (`TasteRecap`, `WeekPlan.tasteChanges`). Demo screens `taste`, `train-taste`.
- **Customise warm-up and cool-down** (#293): in **New workout** (Session flow) and **Quick session**, warm-up and cool-down were always auto-picked; only their length could be dragged. Design A + C from `warmup-cooldown/proposals.png`. In New workout, the **Warm-up ›** and **Cool-down ›** chips now open a sheet (Leave out is in the sheet); Quick session gets **Warm-up** and **Cool-down** cards under Muscle focus. The sheet has **style tiles** (warm-up: Auto, Pulse raiser, Dynamic, Activation; cool-down: Auto, Stretch, Foam roll, Easy cardio, Breathe) and the block's **moves**: swap, remove, add a move (picker opens on warm-up drills or stretches) or change a move's minutes, which turns the block into your own moves (their time sets the block's length; **Back to style** undoes it). **Use as my default** saves the style or moves to the profile (`Profile.warmupStyle` / `cooldownStyle`, own moves as the block's only picks), so new workouts and the plan use them. Per-workout choices are kept on the workout (`CustomWorkout.flowTweaks`) and applied through `Profile.applying(_:)`; quick sessions take them in `Generator.quickSession(…, tweaks:)`. Quick sessions for picked muscles now also get a warm-up and cool-down (4 + 3 min up to 20 min, else 5 + 4, taken from the duration). Auto = plans exactly as before (unit tested). Demo screens `flow-warmup`, `flow-cooldown`, `quick-flow`.
- **Edit a workout's exercises before starting** (#295): only exercises of the generated plan could be swapped; added library workouts, quick sessions, extras, example and shared workouts were fixed lists, and no workout could drop, add or reorder exercises. Design B from `edit-before-start/proposals.png`: the **Exercises** tile (pencil) and a lime **Edit** button above the exercise list open **Edit exercises**, a compact sheet grouped by block: drag ≡ to reorder, red − to remove (rest-time drills go with their exercise, a superset left with one exercise ends, letters renumber), a − sets + stepper, ⇄ swap (same Swap sheet, picks for this workout), **Add to <block>** (exercise picker, starting prescription) and **Back to the workout as planned**; **Save · 66′ · 18 exercises** shows the new length; closing with changes shows the Close + Undo bar (#285). Works for plan days, extras, quick sessions and library workouts; this workout only (plan days for this week, a library workout until it's finished), locked once started. The session page shows **Edited** with **Reset**; card Swap on a library workout or an edited one changes that workout's list. Edits sync with the profile (`SyncedProfile.sessionEdits`, `SessionEdit`; a re-planned session drops its edit). Engine: `SessionEdit.swift` (unit tested). Demo screens `edit-exercises`, `session-edited`.
- **Close + Undo** (#285): closing a screen with unsaved input (X, Cancel, swipe down or back) lost it without a word. Now the screen still closes at once, and a gradient bar (design A from `cancel-confirm/toasts.png`: icon tile, what was closed and what was in it, lime **Undo** in a 6 s countdown ring) brings it back with every value. Untouched screens close silently; saving never shows it. Covers New programme, Edit programme / Make it mine, Build your own exercise, Exercise picker, Gym setup, Log a missed workout, PR attempt (once tries are logged), Log measurements, Away period, Log a climb, Training profile sections, Body & Health, Community profile, Share a win, 2-step setup / new recovery codes (one-time codes come back so they can be saved) and Change email (code sent). The bar sits in its own window above sheets, swipe down to hide it, VoiceOver announces it with an Undo action. `UndoClose.swift` (`UndoOffer`, `CloseGuard`, `.undoableClose`); demo screen `undo-bar`. Audit in `cancel-confirm/audit.md`.
- **New workout: Cancel did nothing** (#285): after Generate, Cancel on New / Edit workout tried to show a "Discard changes?" dialog that never appeared on iOS 26, and swipe-down was blocked, so the sheet couldn't be closed. It now closes at once and shows the same Undo bar.
