## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Visual muscle cards in Explore** (#163): the muscle list under the body map is now one gradient card per muscle (colour per body region, same as Generate from muscles) instead of grey rows. Each card shows a close-up of that muscle on your body figure (M/F), lit in its load colour from this week's plan (white when the plan doesn't load it), the muscle name, how many exercises train it, when you last trained it (Today, Yesterday, 3d ago…), and this week's sets on the right. On a Volume-progression plan the tracked muscles show hard sets against this week's target (e.g. 10/13) with a progress bar. Without a plan the sets are hidden. Group headers, the New exercise card and tap → muscle page are unchanged. Engine: `BodyMapLayout.focus(_:figure:)`. Demo screens `explore-list`, `explore-volume` in CI screenshots.
- **Bigger, easier muscle picker in the exercise builder** (#168): New / Edit exercise shows the front and back figures across the full width (was ~2/3, 240 pt high) and adds **Full screen**: one large figure with a Front / Back switch, legend and picked-muscle chips. Header: round X top left and a lime **Save** button, no longer sharing one capsule. Demo screen `muscle-picker` in CI screenshots.

- **Training methods** (#156): Training profile → **Training method** (also from Account) picks how the plan progresses, separate from the goal. **Block periodization** (default, plans unchanged): Base → Build → Peak → Deload with double progression. **Daily undulating**: each session type rotates Heavy (main 3–5, accessories 6–8, long rests) / Moderate (the goal's range) / Light (main 10–12, accessories 12–15) days; the Peak extra set skips light days. **RIR autoregulated**: logged reps in reserve set the next load (≥ 1 above target → +1 increment, ≥ 2 → +2; ≥ 2 below → −5 %); on a "Take it easier" readiness day today's session drops 1 RPE and one set on accessories and explosive work (main lift kept), shown on the readiness card. **Volume progression**: hard sets per major muscle (chest, lats, mid back, side delts, biceps, triceps, quads, hamstrings, glutes) aim at 10 → 13 → 16 → 6 per week (beginner −20 %, advanced +2) by adding or trimming accessory sets (2–5 per exercise, ≤ 10 per muscle per session, ≤ 115 % session time); exercises unchanged. **Minimal dose**: sessions ≤ 40 min, ≤ 4 strength exercises, 2 hard sets each (RPE 9, beginners 8), supersets by default, also for Generate from muscles. All methods: a lift not logged for ≥ 14 days eases back in at −10 %. The chosen method shows its evidence in short; **Sources for each method** opens the full references per method (authors, title, journal), each linking to a PubMed search for the title. Optional `method` on the profile (older profiles decode). Engine: `TrainingMethod` (+ `sources`), `MethodSource`, `DayZone`, `VolumeTargets`, `Session.easedForReadiness()`, `LoadAdvisor.suggest(method:now:)`. Demo screen `profile-method`.
